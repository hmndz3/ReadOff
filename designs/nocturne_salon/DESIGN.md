## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, literary-competitive environment tailored for passionate readers engaged in asynchronous and head-to-head book duels. Blending the warmth of a late-night private library with the kinetic drive of high-stakes performance tracking, the aesthetic fuses classic editorial bookmanship with modern gamification mechanics.

### Design Movement
**Tactile Nocturnal Editorial:** A hybrid design language merging midnight skeuomorphic warmth (deckled borders, ambient parchment glows, rich book cloth textures) with contemporary, hyper-legible telemetry dashboards. Surfaces evoke dense, dyed book covers and polished mahogany desks under directional amber lamplight.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for bibliophiles, voracious speed-readers, book clubs, and competitive goal-setters. The interface evokes quiet focus, prestigious academic achievement, and the adrenaline of neck-and-neck progression without visual exhaustion or hyper-stimulating neon clutter.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for prolonged nocturnal reading sessions, prioritizing contrast, chromatic meaning, and emotional warmth.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#F5A623` / `#FFB84D`):** Amber Gold. Dedicated exclusively to user-centric achievements, active user progress bars, primary interactive buttons, and victory states.
- **Secondary (`#2DD4BF` / `#14B8A6`):** Electric Cyan. Dedicated to rival/opponent metrics, dual timelines, live clash comparisons, and counter-actions.
- **Neutral Stack:**
  - `Base Background`: `#12151C` (Deep Ink Navy)
  - `Surface Low (Cards)`: `#181D26` (Midnight Buckram)
  - `Surface Medium (Modals/Overlays)`: `#222834` (Smoky Slate)
  - `Borders & Separators`: `#2E3646` (Subtle Book Cloth) to `#3B4457` (Active Stroke)
- **Text & Editorial Accents:**
  - `Primary Text`: `#F5F0EB` (Soft Cream / Unbleached Rag Paper)
  - `Secondary Text`: `#E2D9CE` (Parchment Muted)
  - `Tertiary Text`: `#8E98A8` (Graphite Dust)

## Typography

The type system embodies a conscious dual personality: the unhurried elegance of classic publishing juxtaposed against precise, analytical sports metrics.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for book titles, match announcements, chapters, and victory banners. Evokes the craftsmanship of letterpress and fine bookbindings.
- **Interface & Stats (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, open apertures ensure instant legibility of numeric page counts, reading speed (WPM), pace discrepancies, and system telemetry.
- **Numerical Treatment:** Numeric stats use tabular lining numbers when displayed inside duel progress meters, timers, and leaderboard listings to maintain alignment during real-time score updates.

## Layout & Spacing

This layout model adopts an editorial, column-disciplined grid that respects negative space like broad book margins.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout, max content container width of 1280px with 32px gutters and 48px page margins. Duel telemetry sits in a dominant 8-column arena, paired with a 4-column reading journal and rival side-car feed.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px page margins. Head-to-head metrics transition into a stacked confrontation view.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px page margins. Duels collapse into vertical split tracks, anchoring user and rival progress directly atop one another.

### Spacing Rhythm
Built on an 8px base rhythm (with half-step 4px micro-spacing). Editorial cards utilize expansive inner padding (`card-padding-lg` = 36px) to emulate the breathing room of a printed page.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use clinical grayscale drop shadows; instead, it utilizes warm chromatic ambient lighting, layered dark surfaces, and subtle boundary strokes.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Canvas Base (`#12151C`):** Ground floor; completely static background.
- **Card Surfaces (`#181D26`):** Lifted with a micro-thin, top-weighted border (`1px solid #2E3646`) resembling a leather book spine catching light.
- **Elevated Interactive Objects (`#222834`):** Floating duel cards, active reader popovers, and tactical widgets.

### Ambient Chromatic Glows
- **Amber Player Bloom:** Active components controlled by the user emit a diffuse, low-opacity amber halo (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.12)`).
- **Teal Rival Bloom:** Opponent milestones and counter-leads emit an electric cyan counter-glow (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.10)`).
- **Resting Cards:** Low-contrast outlines without drop shadows during default state, maintaining quiet dignity until action is engaged.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a rounded geometric silhouette (16px base radius on core containers), lending a comfortable, pocket-sized book feel to cards and interactive surfaces.

- **Primary Cards & Modals:** 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg`). Softened corners evoke modern hardcovers and digital tablets.
- **Inner Controls & Mini-Stats:** 8px corner radius (`rounded-md`).
- **Interactive Badges, Pills & Avatars:** Fully rounded pill-shapes (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectangular book covers.
- **Dual Meter Tracks:** 9999px pill tracks to maintain smooth, uninterrupted fluid progression.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Log Pages, Join Duel):** `#F5A623` solid background, `#12151C` bold typography, `rounded-md` (8px). On hover: shifts to `#FFB84D` with an amber underglow.
- **Secondary / Rival Response (Counter Read, Poke):** Ghost button with 1.5px border in `#2DD4BF`, text in `#2DD4BF`. On hover: translucent fill `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary Action:** Borderless `#E2D9CE` text on transparent fill, shifting to `#F5F0EB` with an underline transition.

### Duel Progress Track (Split Race Indicator)
- A dual horizontal or vertical race track.
- The user's channel displays `#F5A623` with real-time gradient fill, while the rival's channel displays `#2DD4BF`.
- The track surface is recessed with `#12151C` base fill, bordered by `#2E3646`.
- Micro-milestone nodes represent book chapters, styled as warm hollow beads that fill with gold or cyan upon completion.

### Cards
- Standard background `#181D26`, stroke `#2E3646`, 16px radius, with 24px inner padding.
- Book cover thumbnails nestle within 8px radius with a slight skeuomorphic inner spine shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pace Badges:** Pill-shaped tags with `0.5rem` padding.
- Fast/Leading: `rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.15)` background with `#FFB84D` text.
- Rival Threat: `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.15)` background with `#2DD4BF` text.
- Literary Genre: `#222834` background with `#E2D9CE` text.

### Inputs & Number Steppers
- Page counter inputs feature large, crisp numbers (`Plus Jakarta Sans 800`) inside a `#12151C` container framed by `#2E3646`.
- Focus state switches border to `#F5A623` with a soft 3px amber focus ring at 20% opacity.
- Incremental buttons (+10, +25, +Chapter) flank the field in muted slate.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Duels
- Custom 20px boxes rounded to 6px. Checked state fills with `#F5A623` with an ink-dark SVG checkmark.
- Segmented Match Selectors ("Daily Sprint", "Book Marathon", "Chapter Sprint") use a tab-bar enclosure with an animated sliding thumb in `#222834`.
